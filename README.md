# Dark Moments of Solstice

## ❄️ Overview

Dark Moments of Solstice is a story-driven visual novel that combines mystery, adventure, comedy, and puzzle-solving into a globe-spanning journey. Players take control of Masato, who must travel across the world to investigate a series of mysterious bombs before they can unleash devastating consequences.

Each bomb is locked behind a different trivia challenge. From geography and history to music, sport, and cultural knowledge, every puzzle provides clues that determine which wire should be cut. As the journey progresses, the bombs become more sophisticated, introducing new mechanics, additional wires, and increasingly difficult clues.

Between missions, the story alternates between tense investigations and light-hearted moments, featuring a colourful cast of allies and villains whose personalities add humour and mystery to the adventure. Every chapter takes place in a different part of the world, with its own atmosphere, scenery, and puzzle theme.

As more bombs are discovered, Masato begins to realise that the incidents may not be isolated at all. What starts as a worldwide rescue mission slowly grows into something much larger, forcing him to uncover the truth before time runs out.

## 🎭 Story Summary

In 2034, unexplained bombs begin appearing across the globe. Each device is unlike any conventional explosive, presenting a category and a clue instead of a countdown. The only way to safely disarm a bomb is to correctly interpret its puzzle and cut the appropriate wire.

Guided by Raviolo through radio communications, Masato embarks on an international mission that takes him from one country to the next, facing new environments, increasingly challenging puzzles, and unexpected obstacles at every stop. Along the way, he encounters strange clues, recurring messages, and a mysterious organisation that always seems to be one step ahead.

While Masato races to prevent each disaster, the story periodically shifts perspective to the villains, offering humorous glimpses into their headquarters and hinting that their plans extend far beyond the individual bombs. As the mystery deepens, seemingly unrelated events begin to connect, revealing that the worldwide crisis is only part of a much greater scheme.

With every successful mission, the stakes continue to rise. The puzzles become more demanding, the choices more difficult, and the journey more dangerous, building toward a dramatic conclusion where the fate of the world depends on the decisions Masato makes.

## 🧪 The Compound: Heliohydronate–Argentate (Type Ω-1)
- **Formula:** `C₁₇H₂₆O₁₀He₃Ag⁺`  
- **Aliases:** *Cryostatic Heliohydronate–Argentate*, *Type Ω-1 Zero-Kelvin Condensate*  
- **Effect:** Converts energy into inverse heat, freezing both matter and the flow of reality.  
- **First Activation:** Core Vessel Prototype Test, conducted by King Ted’s minions under royal supervision.

## 🕹️ Gameplay
A dark fantasy visual novel combining atmospheric storytelling with branching choices.  
Features include:
- Dialogue-driven cutscenes  
- Consequential moral decisions  
- Ever changing gameplay with multiple wires sharing one way to die.
